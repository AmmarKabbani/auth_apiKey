# LiftLog — Application Brief

## What it is
**LiftLog** is a simple, native iPhone app for following a fixed **4-day Upper/Lower
split** training program and logging your progress in the gym. It's built to do two
things well: show you today's workout, and help you beat last time.

## Who it's for
A lifter following a structured Upper/Lower routine who wants a fast, no-friction way
to record each set — without spreadsheets, ads, accounts, or internet.

## The problem it solves
Progressive overload only works if you remember what you did last time. LiftLog
**pre-fills every set with your previous session's weight and reps**, so you always
know the exact number to match or beat.

## What you can do
- **Browse the program** — 4 ready-made days (Upper A, Lower A, Upper B, Lower B) with
  every exercise and its target sets × reps.
- **Run a session** — open a day, log weight and reps per set, tick each one off. A
  **rest timer** starts automatically when you complete a set.
- **Track progress** — per-exercise strength charts over time and an automatic
  personal-record (PR) badge.
- **Review history** — every finished session, browsable and deletable by date.
- **Choose your units** — switch between kg and lb anytime; past logs convert
  automatically.

## Key principles
- **Fast:** three taps from opening the app to logging your first set.
- **Private & offline:** all data lives on your device — no account, no network, no tracking.
- **Native:** built with SwiftUI for a clean, responsive iPhone experience.

## Tech at a glance
SwiftUI · SwiftData (on-device storage) · Swift Charts · iOS 17+ · no third-party
dependencies.
